Crest Download

What changed, version by version.

Crest is a small notch for the top of your Windows screen. Here is everything that has been added since the first version.

1.4 latest

Small touches everywhere, and a fix for the volume.

  • Fixed: scrolling on the notch now shows the volume, and pop-ups also appear while the notch is open.
  • Tools page: calculator and unit converter, screen color picker, countdown.
  • To-do list next to the quick note.
  • Download alerts: progress bubble, a pop-up when it is done, and the file lands on the shelf.
  • Rain alert and low-battery alerts for Bluetooth devices.
  • Join button for Teams, Zoom, Meet and Webex meetings.
  • Pins: favourite apps and files on the shelf, and pinned clipboard texts.
  • Look: blurred cover behind the player, song progress line, idle buddy, night dimming.
  • Focus stats, keyboard control, hiding during presentations, lower memory use while idle.

1.3

Your whole desk, one notch away.

  • Sound page: switch speakers or headphones, and a volume slider for every app.
  • Controls page: dark mode, Bluetooth, Wi-Fi, keep awake, pause pop-ups, mute mic, focus.
  • Mic mute in every app with Win + Alt + M.
  • Focus timer with work and break rounds.
  • Album cover in the closed notch, smoother icon and clock animations.
  • Screenshots go to the shelf, and copied images show in the clipboard list.
  • Four visualizer styles and four new color presets.
  • Caps Lock and battery pop-ups, internet speed, lyrics timing buttons, settings backup, welcome tour.
  • Colored icons in the notch and in Settings.

1.2

Way more than a music notch.

  • Synced lyrics from LRCLIB.
  • Live activities: charger, headphones, meetings, timer done.
  • Pages: Music, Lyrics, Calendar, Weather, System, Timer, Shelf, Notes.
  • File shelf: drag files in, drag them out later.
  • Split island for a running timer, and privacy dots for camera and microphone.
  • Ambient glow in the cover's colors that pulses with the music.
  • Gestures, a notch on every monitor, per-app rules.

1.1

The big polish pass.

  • Completely new settings app with dark and light themes and a live preview.
  • Follows the app that is actually playing; click the song to open it.
  • Real volume slider, scrolling titles, song-change peek.
  • Colors from album art, theme presets, optional real spectrum.
  • Sharp on every screen scale, and a choice of monitor.

1.0

The first version.

  • A notch at the top of the screen with the time and spectrum bars.
  • Hover to open: title, artist, progress, previous / play / next.
  • Settings window, tray icon, start with Windows.
